Privacy and terms

Not a medical device

aorti.ca is not a medical device. It is a personal-use tool for tracking heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV). It has no role in diagnosing, treating, preventing or monitoring any condition.

Nothing shown in the live monitor, the session viewer, or any exported report is a medical interpretation or medical advice. Summaries are estimates from a consumer chest strap and are for personal context only.

If you have symptoms that concern you, talk to a qualified healthcare professional. Do not make health decisions based on what this app shows you.

What the app is for

  • Recording your own heart rate and RR-interval data from a Bluetooth chest strap.
  • Showing that data live in a widget and, if you turn it on, sharing it to an OBS stream or to people you give your broadcast key to.
  • Storing the recordings as CSV files on your device or in your own Google Drive, at your choice.
  • Summaries so you can look back at your own data with more context than a raw chart.

What the app is not for

  • Diagnosing, ruling out or managing any health condition.
  • Detecting medical emergencies. Do not rely on it to tell you about a dangerous heart rate.
  • Replacing medical-grade ECG, Holter, or clinical HRV measurement.
  • Giving medical advice. Labels in the viewer describe patterns in your data. They are not diagnoses.

Privacy

Your heart data

Recordings stay on your device. If you sign in with Google, the app also saves them to a folder named "HR Monitor Sessions" in your own Google Drive. The app asks only for Drive's drive.file permission, so it can see the files it created and nothing else in your Drive.

I do not run a database of user data, and I never receive your recordings.

Google sign-in

Sign-in is only for Drive. It asks for your email address and access to the app's own Drive files. To keep you signed in, the aorti.ca sign-in service swaps Google's one-time code for tokens. The long-lived token stays in an HttpOnly cookie in your browser, or in the Android Keystore in the app; the service does not store it. Signing out clears the access token.

Live sharing

Share live HR is off until you turn it on. While it is on, the widget's live readings pass through a relay at hr-relay.nakauri.partykit.dev to your OBS overlay or anyone with your broadcast key. The relay keeps each message in memory only long enough to pass it on, and stores nothing.

Analytics

The web pages use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views. It never receives heart data. The Android app leaves it out.

Nothing is sold

I do not sell or share your data, and there are no ads.

Other services

  • Google for sign-in and the Drive API, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts on the monitor and viewer pages. The font request carries normal browser headers.
  • PartyKit (Cloudflare Workers) runs the relay for Share live HR.
  • GitHub hosts the source code and the Android APK. The home page asks GitHub for the latest APK version.
  • Vercel hosts aorti.ca and the sign-in service.
  • Web3Forms delivers the contact form to my inbox.
  • api.qrserver.com draws the PayPal QR code on the home page from the public PayPal link.

Deleting your data

  • Google Drive. Delete the "HR Monitor Sessions" folder, then remove aorti.ca's access in your Google account settings.
  • Android. Clear cache, under Local session cache in the app's diagnostics, deletes the recordings stored on the phone, including any not yet on Drive.
  • Browser. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes recordings kept in browser storage. Files saved to a folder you picked stay there until you delete them.
  • Messages. Ask through the contact form and I will delete your message.

Check it yourself

The source code is public on GitHub, so you can read exactly what the app does with your data.
