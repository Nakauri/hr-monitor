Changelog

What changed in aorti.ca, newest first.

October 12, 2026

New home page and changelog Site

The home page has a cleaner layout, newer screenshots and a stream preview at true size. Every update since launch now has its own page you can filter, and the privacy page is finished.

Tidier monitor controls Monitor

Stop, posture, the Log row and live sharing now sit in one Controls section, closed until you need it, and its header still shows your posture and whether you are live. Switches, buttons and fields match the rest of aorti.

Saved sessions show first Viewer

The viewer opens straight to what you saw last, then checks Drive quietly in the background. It only redraws if something actually changed.

Correct session lengths Viewer

Some sessions showed impossible lengths, like 70 hours. Times now come from the real clock in each file, and a new web session always starts its own clock.

Arrange your Trends Viewer

Pick which Trends cards show and drag them into your own order. Each one can be half or full width.

Smoother zooming Viewer

Scrolling and zooming the Day chart is much lighter on phones and laptops. On touch, pinch or double-tap to zoom; on a laptop, drag across the chart to zoom to that stretch.

Tidier Day view Viewer

The date now opens a calendar for picking another day, and one Layers list turns chart lines on or off. Markers open in a drawer that no longer squeezes the chart.

Bubble stays on screen Android app

The floating bubble can no longer be dragged or rotated partly off the edge of the screen.

October 11, 2026

Day view explorer Viewer

The Day view now shows the whole day on one chart you can explore. Marks show as diamonds you can edit or delete, and sleep start and end can be marked by hand.

Phone viewer shows your data Viewer

The viewer in the app could open empty on some phones. It now shows your saved summaries right away and refreshes them in the background.

Log a symptom or a note Monitor

A Log row lets you mark a symptom or jot a quick note during a session. Both show on the Day view at that minute.

Symptom from the notification Android app

The recording notification has a Symptom button for marking a moment. Its status now reads in hours and minutes.

Choose when to sync Monitor

On the web, Drive sync is now a setting you can turn off. After you stop, the monitor says where the session was saved and links straight to it.

Bubble closes with the session Android app

The floating bubble closes by itself once no session is running. Its stop icon is easier to see.

October 10, 2026

Recording comes back on its own Android app

If Android closes the app while you record, the session picks back up by itself, in the same file, usually within a few seconds.

Reconnects when back in range Android app

Walk away from your phone and the notification says the strap is reconnecting. Come back and recording picks up again by itself, with no need to open the app.

Hourly files on Drive Android app

Sessions now also save as small hourly files in a folder for each month on your Drive. Each finished hour uploads once, so syncing is faster.

Refreshed stream widget Stream overlay

The widget has a new font and clearer warning colours, and a warning keeps flashing until it clears. The OBS source now matches the monitor preview at the same size.

October 9, 2026

Faster sync, no duplicate files Android app

Sync no longer reads every saved session again, and a resumed session no longer leaves a second copy on Drive.

No more stuck restore screen Android app

Coming back to the app no longer shows a spinner for up to a minute, and Stop responds right away, even on a weak connection. Long sessions ask before stopping.

September 13, 2026

No more hanging on restore Android app

In rare cases the app could sit on Restoring session until you force-closed it. It now finishes or gives up within about a minute.

August 25, 2026

Overlay stays live all session Stream overlay

Late in long sessions the stream overlay and Watch live could stop updating. They now keep going for the whole session.

July 5, 2026

Safer sign-in on the phone Android app

Sign-in details are stored more securely on the phone, and live data from other devices is checked before it shows.

May 23, 2026

No double stream on reopen Android app

Reopening the app while you share live no longer starts a second copy of your stream.

May 21, 2026

More dependable Drive sync Viewer

Sync now waits for an upload to land before it says it is done, and new data shows in the viewer right after. Sync messages are plainer too.

May 17, 2026

Floating heart rate bubble Android app

A long press on the widget pops your heart rate into a bubble that floats over other apps. A tap switches it between a compact view and a card with HRV.

May 15, 2026

Markers on the right day Viewer

Markers from sessions that cross midnight now land on the correct day.

May 9, 2026

Full 24-hour day chart Viewer

The Day chart now runs midnight to midnight, and sessions longer than a day show on every day they cover. Early-morning sessions no longer slip to the day before.

May 3, 2026

Keeps recording overnight Android app

Android is much less likely to stop a recording overnight. If it does, the app brings it back on its own.

April 29, 2026

Choose your summary stats Viewer

Pick which numbers show in a session summary from its menu. SDNN is now one of the options.

April 27, 2026

Faster charts Viewer

The viewer redraws far less, so charts scroll and resize smoothly. Zoomed-out charts keep their highs and lows, so they match the summary numbers.

April 26, 2026

Try the viewer with sample data Viewer

Open Sample data in the menu to see what the viewer looks like with two weeks of sample sessions. No sign-in needed.

Plays nicely with Sleep As Android Android app

aorti can share your strap with sleep-tracking apps. No need to disconnect one to use the other.

Mobile reliability fixes Android app

Recovers better from brief strap drops, including overnight. Long sessions use less battery.

Viewer tweaks Viewer

The chills sensitivity slider filters events properly. Sundays open in the week view, and HR shifts show in purple so they do not look like chills.

April 25, 2026

Save CSV anywhere Android app

Tapping Export CSV opens a save dialog, so the file goes wherever you pick.

Record without signing in Android app

You can record without a Google account; sessions stay on the phone. Old ones are cleared before storage runs low, and you can clear them yourself.

April 24, 2026

Swipe to stop Android app

Ending a session is now a swipe, so a stray tap will not stop it. Stop in the notification works again.

April 23, 2026

Fewer false alerts Monitor

Chills and flushing alerts were rewritten and fire far less often on ordinary changes in heart rate.

Late-night sessions split by day Viewer

A session that runs past midnight now shows its part on each day instead of all on the day it started.

April 21, 2026

Now called aorti Site

The app has a new name and a home at aorti.ca. You can sign in right from the home page.

Steadier strap connection Android app

The Android app was rebuilt to hold a steadier connection to your strap, so readings no longer go quiet mid-session.

April 19, 2026

Record in the background Android app

Start a session on Android and it keeps recording with the screen off. A settings shortcut helps stop your phone from closing it to save battery.

Skipped-beat detection Monitor

The monitor flags beats that look skipped or early, with a slider to set how sensitive it is.

April 18, 2026

Reports by day, week and month Viewer

The viewer turns your sessions into plain-English reports with noteworthy moments called out. It syncs both ways with Google Drive.

Heart rate on your stream Stream overlay

Add the overlay as a browser source in OBS and it mirrors the widget on your monitor as you change its settings. Watch live shows the same view on any other screen.

Free, no ads, no account needed. Not a medical tool.
