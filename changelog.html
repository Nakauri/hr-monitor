Changelog

What changed in aorti.ca, newest first.

October 10, 2026

Recording comes back on its own

If Android closes the app while you record, Aorti picks the session back up by itself, in the same file, usually within a few seconds.

Reconnects when you are back in range

Walk away from your phone and the notification says the strap is reconnecting. Come back and recording picks up again by itself, with no need to open the app.

Hourly files on Drive

Sessions now also save as small hourly files in a folder for each month on your Drive. Each finished hour uploads once, so syncing is faster.

October 9, 2026

Faster sync, no duplicate files

Sync no longer reads every saved session again, and a resumed session no longer leaves a second copy on Drive.

No more stuck restore screen

Coming back to the app no longer shows a spinner for up to a minute, and Stop responds right away, even on a weak connection. Long sessions ask before stopping.

April 26, 2026

Try the viewer with sample data

Open Sample data in the menu to see what the viewer looks like with two weeks of fake sessions. No sign-in needed.

Plays nicely with Sleep As Android

Aorti can share your strap with sleep-tracking apps. No need to disconnect one to use the other.

Mobile reliability fixes

Recovers better from brief strap drops, including overnight. Long sessions use less battery.

Viewer tweaks

Chills sensitivity slider filters events properly. Sundays open in the week view. HR shifts show in purple so they don't look like chills.

April 25, 2026

Save CSV anywhere

Tapping Export CSV opens a save dialog, so the file goes wherever you pick.

Free, no ads, no account needed. Not a medical tool.
